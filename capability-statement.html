Heightened Senses, LLC is an SBA-certified SDVOSB and U.S. Air Force prime contractor providing UAS and counter-UAS integration, operator training, and advisory to federal, Department of War, state, local, and public safety buyers. We start with the requirement, design the architecture, select and integrate compliant systems, train the operators, and sustain the capability. We are an integrator, not a manufacturer, and we document compliance status and country of origin for every item we quote.

Core Competencies
Counter-UAS

C-UAS Integration & Airspace Awareness

Site survey, layered detection architecture (radar, passive RF, acoustic, EO/IR, Remote ID), system interfaces, installation, operator training, and sustainment for fixed sites, water treatment, power plants, data centers, ports, vehicles, and correctional facilities. Mitigation for agencies holding that authority. Purchase or lease.

UAS

UAS Procurement & Mission Integration

Mission-to-configuration selection, NDAA and Blue UAS documentation by configuration, sourcing through executed OEM channels, integration, and operator onboarding. ISR, FPV, hybrid VTOL, avionics, and ground control. Part 107 aerial data services available.

Services

Training, Advisory & Program Support

Operator and proficiency training; requirements and program planning (CONOPS, policy, SOPs, sustainment); NDAA and ASDA procurement and compliance review for agencies buying and manufacturers entering the federal market. Defined scope, written deliverables.
