Heightened Senses is a UAS and counter-UAS integrator, authorized reseller, and advisory practice serving federal, Department of War, and public safety buyers. We select and integrate NDAA-compliant systems against the requirement, train the operators, and support the program after fielding. We are not a manufacturer, and we document clearance and country of origin for every item we quote.

Core Competencies
Counter-UAS

C-UAS Integration & Airspace Awareness

Site survey, sensing requirements, layered detection architecture (radar, passive RF, EO/IR, Remote ID), system interfaces, installation, operator training, and sustainment. Handheld mitigation for authorized federal customers. Purchase or lease.

UAS

UAS Procurement & Mission Integration

Mission-to-configuration selection, NDAA and Blue UAS documentation by configuration, sourcing through executed OEM channels, integration, and operator onboarding. ISR, FPV, hybrid VTOL, avionics, and ground control. Part 107 aerial data services available.

Services

Training, Advisory & Program Support

Operator and proficiency training; requirements and program planning (CONOPS, policy, SOPs, sustainment); NDAA and ASDA procurement and compliance review for agencies buying and manufacturers entering the federal market. Defined scope, written deliverables.
