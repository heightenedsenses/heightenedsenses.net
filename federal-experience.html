COUNTER-SUAS RADAR LEASE

United States Air Force · 20th Fighter Wing · Shaw AFB, SC
Counter-sUAS Radar System Lease
Prime Contractor · Contract FA480326P0068 · Awarded September 2026

Heightened Senses competed for this requirement under a total small business set-aside and won it as prime. We are delivering two Robin Radar IRIS 3D counter-sUAS radar systems under a lease, with site survey, installation, network integration, spectrum coordination, operator training, and sustainment support.

The contract carries a one-year base period and two priced option years exercisable at the Government's discretion. Current stage: awarded, delivery in progress.
