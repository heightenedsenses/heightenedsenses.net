Drone Detection for Critical Infrastructure

Drones loiter over and probe critical infrastructure: water and wastewater treatment plants, power plants and substations, data centers, pipelines, and ports. Perimeter fences were not built for an aerial threat. We design a layered detection architecture for the facility, install and integrate it with the security operation already in place, and train the people who run it, matched to the authority the operator and its law enforcement partners hold.

The Architecture

Layers for the Facility

A single sensor is a gap, not a program. Each layer fills a gap the others leave, fused into one airspace picture the security operation can act on.

RDR

Radar

Non-cooperative detection and tracking of small UAS, cuing EO/IR sensors and feeding a unified common operating picture.

RF

RF Spectrum Sensing

Passive detection and characterization of UAS control and video links. No emissions.

AC

Acoustic Detection

Passive microphone arrays that detect and locate small UAS by acoustic signature, including drones that emit no RF. Strongest at short range and low altitude.

EO

EO/IR Confirmation

Visual classification and confirmation of detected tracks for informed response decisions, day and night.

RID

Remote ID Monitoring

Broadcast Remote ID sensing integrated into the airspace picture for identification and pattern-of-life awareness.

Can a critical infrastructure operator stop or jam a drone?
Mitigation is restricted to organizations and personnel possessing applicable legal authority, and is subject to federal requirements governing authorized systems, training, certification, and employment. A facility typically plans its response with the law enforcement agency that holds that authority.
What sensors does a treatment plant, substation, or data center need?
Usually more than one. Radar provides non-cooperative detection and tracking, passive RF and Remote ID help identify the aircraft and its control link, acoustic sensing adds a passive check at short range, and EO/IR confirms what the other layers report. The site survey sets the mix.
Can drone detection be leased for a fixed facility?
Yes. Fixed-site detection can be quoted as a purchase or a lease. The lease structure gives a facility detection capability without a capital acquisition, with sustainment priced up front.
Will it work with our existing security systems?
IRIS supports SAPIENT, ASTERIX, CoT, and XML data interfaces for integration into common operating pictures, including the systems a facility already runs.
