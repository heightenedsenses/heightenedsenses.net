Common Questions

Questions Buyers Ask

Can drone detection be mounted on a vehicle?
Yes. Vehicle kits deliver detection and mitigation on the move, and the IRIS 3D radar is on-the-move capable. We match the kit to the vehicle, the mission, and the operating picture it has to feed.
Is there a passive option that does not emit?
Yes. The DTI System is a wearable, portable passive system that detects, tracks, and identifies aerial threats without emitting.
Can a state or local agency use mobile mitigation?
Only with the authority to do so. The SAFER SKIES Act amended 6 U.S.C. § 124n to extend detection and mitigation authority to state, local, tribal, and territorial law enforcement under a federal framework of authorized systems, training, and certification. We confirm the authority your agency holds before we quote a mitigation system.
Do you certify counter-UAS operators?
No. Under 6 CFR Part 124, the FBI National Counter-UAS Training Center is the certifying authority. We provide operator and program proficiency training, coordinated with manufacturer training where the OEM requires it.
