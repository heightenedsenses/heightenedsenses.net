Our Approach

THE REQUIREMENT SELECTS THE SYSTEM.

We start with the operator, the mission, and the threat or operational problem. Then we work through the operating environment, the authorities that apply, the interfaces the system has to fit, the budget, and the acquisition constraints. Only then do we recommend technology.

We hold relationships across many manufacturers and are tied to none of them. When the right answer is a technology we do not sell, we will tell you, and we will help you find it.

How We Work

MISSION TO ACQUISITION

We can stay with a requirement from the first definition of the mission through fielding, training, procurement, and sustainment.

  1. 01

    Mission

    Understand the operational requirement, environment, constraints, authorities, and desired outcome.

  2. 02

    Architecture

    Determine the sensing, aircraft, communications, C2, payload, integration, and sustainment requirements appropriate to the mission.

  3. 03

    Technology

    Evaluate and source appropriate compliant technologies and configuration-specific documentation.

  4. 04

    Operator

    Coordinate integration, fielding, onboarding, proficiency training, and operational implementation.

  5. 05

    Acquisition

    Support the requirement through quotation, federal fulfillment, documentation, invoicing, and sustainment.

FIELD DEMONSTRATIONS
