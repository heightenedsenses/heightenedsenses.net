About

MISSION-INFORMED
INTEGRATION

Heightened Senses is platform agnostic and system agnostic. We are centric to the warfighter and the public safety professional we are honored to serve. The requirement selects the system. We do not carry a manufacturer's inventory into a room and look for a mission to fit it. We start with the operator, the threat, the authority environment, and the budget, and we recommend what actually serves them, including when the answer is a platform we do not sell.

Heightened Senses, LLC was founded in the spirit of “Service after Service” by a Purple Heart recipient and retired USMC Gunnery Sergeant whose career spanned infantry, surveillance and countersurveillance, and intelligence. We are an authorized reseller, systems integrator, and advisory practice for American-made uncrewed systems and counter-UAS, with a specialty in NDAA compliance. We list only manufacturers that have authorized us to represent them, and nothing else. Those authorizations are the result of our standard, not a substitute for it.

We source compliant platforms, deliver them, and stay on the requirement through installation, operator training, and sustainment. We are a prime contractor to the United States Air Force, and we bring the same judgment to a single-aircraft public safety quote that we bring to a multi-year federal lease. Not just hardware. Mission-informed judgment at every engagement.

Consultancy

ADVISORY PRACTICE

Heightened Senses is a consultancy as well as a contractor. Before we sell anything, we are often the people an agency calls to figure out what it actually needs, whether it is allowed to have it, and how to buy it without a protest or a compliance finding. We have done that work for federal installations, a state National Guard formation, a federal land management agency, municipal police departments, and a public school district building its first UAS program.

Our specialty is NDAA compliance: what the American Security Drone Act and the FY2020 through FY2026 NDAA provisions actually prohibit, how the Blue UAS framework and the DIU cleared list work in practice, what the Authorized Systems List means for counter-UAS, and how to read a bill of materials for a covered component. We advise agencies buying and manufacturers entering the federal market, and we maintain a current-checked register of more than 900 federal, state, and local UAS and counter-UAS authorities to do it from.

How we engage

  • Scoped engagements with a defined deliverable: a compliance determination, a program design, a procurement strategy, a policy package
  • Retained advisory for agencies and manufacturers who need a standing resource rather than a one-time answer
  • Embedded support inside a procurement, from market research response through award, including the questions to the contracting officer that shape the requirement
  • Independent of any product sale. If the answer is a platform we do not carry, or no platform at all, that is the answer you get

Procurement Path

HOW TO BUY

Heightened Senses is an SBA-Certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business. That gives a contracting officer a compliant path to award that supports the agency's small business and Veteran-owned goals in the same action, and it gives a public safety agency a vendor who has already been through a federal buy. One accountable contractor from quote through sustainment. You do not manage the manufacturer. We do.

01

SDVOSB Set-Aside & Sole Source

FAR 19.14 set-aside and sole-source authorities allow award to an SDVOSB without full and open competition where the requirement fits the applicable thresholds and the price is determined fair and reasonable. We build the quote and the documentation so that determination is easy to make.

02

Simplified Acquisition

Clean quotes structured for simplified acquisition procedures and purchase card thresholds. Requirements in, quote out, with lead times you can plan against. WAWF and PIEE invoicing supported.

03

Lease Instead of Buy

Multi-year lease with option years priced up front and sustainment included, for fixed-site systems. Puts capability on site without a capital acquisition. The Air Force chose this model for our radar contract at Shaw.

04

State & Local Purchase Orders

Direct purchase orders for public safety agencies, with grant-funded acquisitions supported end to end including the documentation. Manufacturer list pricing as the baseline, formal quotations on request, and lease options on select fixed-site systems.
