How We Work

MISSION TO ACQUISITION

We stay with a requirement from the first definition of the mission through fielding, training, procurement, and sustainment. The order matters: the technology is chosen in step three, not step one.

  1. 01

    Mission

    Define

    The mission, the site, the threat, the operating environment, and the authority you operate under.

  2. 02

    Architecture

    Design

    Sensors, aircraft, effectors, networks, and interfaces, and how they fit what you already run.

  3. 03

    Technology

    Select

    Platforms chosen against the architecture, with NDAA, Blue UAS, and country of origin documented by configuration. If the right answer is a system we do not carry, we say so.

  4. 04

    Operator

    Field & Train

    Installation, integration, operator training, and sustainment after fielding.

  5. 05

    Acquisition

    Procure

    We can execute the purchase as a federal prime contractor and SBA-certified SDVOSB, with the documentation the contract file needs.
