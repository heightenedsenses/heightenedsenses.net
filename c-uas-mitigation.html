Mitigation Systems

Response starts with alerting and a pre-planned operations plan. Mitigation is one part of response, and only for agencies that hold the authority. Before we quote a mitigation system, we confirm the authority your agency holds, the training and certification that authority requires, and how the system fits the detection architecture around it.
