FOUNDER-LED ACCOUNTABILITY

The requirement selects the system. We start with the operator, the threat, the authority environment, and the budget, and we recommend what serves them, including when the answer is a platform we do not sell.

Heightened Senses, LLC was founded in July 2023 in the spirit of “Service after Service”. Paul “Buck” Chambers is a retired U.S. Marine Corps Gunnery Sergeant with a background in counterintelligence and HUMINT. He leads the company’s UAS and counter-UAS procurement, integration, training, and advisory work.

We source compliant platforms, deliver them, and stay on the requirement through installation, operator training, and sustainment, with the same judgment on a single-aircraft public safety quote as on a federal lease.
