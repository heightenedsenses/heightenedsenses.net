About

Mission-Informed. Operator-Led.

Heightened Senses is a UAS and counter-UAS integrator built by an operator, for operators and the people who equip them. We are not a manufacturer. We are the team that helps you decide what to field, then fields it with you.

What We Believe

The Requirement Selects the System.

Technology decisions made before the mission is understood tend to fail in the field. We start with the operator, the mission, the threat or problem, the environment, and the authorities that apply. The system comes last, and it is whatever serves that requirement best, including when the answer is a platform we do not sell.

Our Approach

From Requirement to Execution

  1. 01

    Understand the Requirement

    We define the mission, the site, the constraints, and the authority before anything else.

  2. 02

    Evaluate the Technology

    We compare candidate systems against that requirement, with compliance and country of origin documented by configuration.

  3. 03

    Integrate the Solution

    We install, configure, and connect the system to what the customer already runs.

  4. 04

    Train the Operator

    We train the people who will run it, coordinated with the manufacturer where required.

  5. 05

    Support Execution

    We stay through procurement, fielding, and sustainment.

Founder

Why Operator Experience Matters

Heightened Senses was founded in July 2023 in the spirit of “Service after Service.” Founder Paul “Buck” Chambers served 16 years in the U.S. Marine Corps. He began as an infantry assaultman with deployments to Iraq and Afghanistan, then spent roughly a decade in counterintelligence and HUMINT, retiring as a Gunnery Sergeant and CI/HUMINT Chief at II Marine Expeditionary Force. He is an FAA Part 107 remote pilot.

That background shapes how we work. An operator learns to ask what the mission actually requires before trusting any piece of equipment, and to judge a system by how it performs in the hands of the people who carry it.

Clear Lines

Integrator. Not a Manufacturer.

Heightened Senses is an integrator, authorized reseller, operator trainer, and advisory practice. We are not a manufacturer and do not represent ourselves as one. We disclose which manufacturers we represent before we advise, and we keep advisory and supply separate where federal rules require it.
