How to Buy

PurchasingInformation

We quote directly to federal, state, and local buyers. The contracting officer determines the acquisition approach; we provide the documentation that supports it.

Procurement Path

HOW TO BUY

01

Direct Quote & PO

Formal quotes with product documentation, country of origin, and lead times. WAWF and PIEE invoicing for federal orders.

02

SDVOSB Eligibility

Heightened Senses is an SBA-certified SDVOSB. Eligibility is not a contract vehicle; set-aside use is the contracting officer's determination.

03

Purchase or Lease

Fixed-site systems can be quoted as a purchase or a lease, with sustainment priced into the acquisition.

04

State & Local

Direct purchase orders for public safety agencies, with documentation for grant-funded acquisitions.

Contract vehicles: Heightened Senses does not currently hold a GSA schedule, BPA, IDIQ, or cooperative purchasing contract.
