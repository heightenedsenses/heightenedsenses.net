Funding and Authority

SAFER SKIES Act
& FEMA Counter-UAS Funding

Two things changed the state and local counter-UAS landscape. In December 2025, Congress enacted the SAFER SKIES Act as part of the FY2026 National Defense Authorization Act (Pub. L. 119-60, Division H, Title LXXXVI), amending 6 U.S.C. § 124n to extend counter-UAS detection and mitigation authority to state, local, tribal, and territorial law enforcement under a federal framework of authorized systems, training, and certification. Alongside it, FEMA designated $250 million in preparedness grant funding for SLTT counter-UAS capability ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Authority and money arrived together. What most agencies lack is a contractor who can turn both into a fielded program.

  • Requirement definition and detection architecture design scoped to grant program criteria and to the authority your agency actually holds
  • Quote packages and sole-source justifications built for grant-funded procurement
  • Policy and operations-plan support aligned to 6 U.S.C. § 124n, the Authorized Systems List, and your state's implementing framework
  • Training and sustainment priced into the acquisition, not discovered after fielding
