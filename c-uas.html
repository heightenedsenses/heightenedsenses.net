Funding Pathways

FEMA C-UAS Grant Program
& Safer Skies Act

Federal funding and expanded state and local authorities have changed the counter-UAS acquisition landscape. The FEMA Counter-UAS Grant Program funds state, local, tribal, and territorial agencies to detect, track, and mitigate unlawful or threatening drones, and the Safer Skies Act expands law enforcement authority to address drone threats to public safety. Major events on U.S. soil are accelerating both timelines.
