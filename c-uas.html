Authority

Detection Is Not Mitigation

Detection and Airspace Awareness

Passive RF, radar, acoustic, EO/IR, and Remote ID detection are available to federal, state, and local agencies. What an agency may operate still depends on the technology, the configuration, and its authority.

Mitigation

Disrupting or defeating a drone is limited to agencies that hold that authority under federal law, with the authorized systems, training, and certification it requires.

The Requirement Selects the System

How We Build a
Counter-UAS Program

A single sensor is a gap, not a program. Every system we deliver comes with the site assessment, authority mapping, policy, training, and sustainment required to field it as an operational capability and defend it in an audit.

Site Assessment

Site survey, threat characterization, layered sensor architecture, siting, power, network, and spectrum coordination. Site survey and installation are part of our contracted scope at Shaw AFB.

Authority Mapping

Before we quote, we map your requirement to the authority your agency identifies: 10 U.S.C. § 130i for covered Department of War facilities, 6 U.S.C. § 124n and the SAFER SKIES framework for SLTT agencies, and the detection options your agency may consider. We raise authority questions before the purchase, not after fielding, and we recommend each agency confirm its operating authority with its own counsel.

Policy & OPLAN Support

Policy development and operations-plan support for agencies pursuing detection programs and certification pathways, including standing plans for fixed sites and event overlays.

Operator Training

Operator and program proficiency training for detection systems and airspace awareness operations, coordinated with manufacturer training where the OEM requires it. We do not issue counter-UAS certifications; the FBI National Counter-UAS Training Center is the certifying authority.

Sustainment

Spares, batteries, software licensing, and lifecycle sustainment sourced with documented, NDAA-compliant supply chains, quoted alongside the system, not discovered after fielding.

Regulatory Baseline

A maintained, current-checked register of more than 900 federal, state, and local UAS and counter-UAS authorities, statutes, policies, and procurement rules. Your program is designed against the actual rules. Available as a standalone advisory engagement for agencies and for manufacturers entering the federal market.
