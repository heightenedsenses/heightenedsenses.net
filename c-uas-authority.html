Authority Mapping

Which Side of the Line Your Requirement Sits On

Covered Federal Facilities

Department of War facilities operate under 10 U.S.C. § 130i. DHS and DOJ components operate under 6 U.S.C. § 124n. Mitigation is available to authorized federal customers within that authority.

State, Local, Tribal, Territorial

The SAFER SKIES Act amended 6 U.S.C. § 124n to extend detection and mitigation authority to SLTT law enforcement under a federal framework of authorized systems, training, and certification.

Detection-Only Programs

Passive RF and radar detection are available to state and local agencies. What an agency may operate still depends on the technology, the configuration, and its authority.

We tell you which side of that line your requirement sits on before we quote it, and what you cannot legally do before you buy something that does it.
Funding and Authority

SAFER SKIES Act
& FEMA Counter-UAS Funding

Two things changed the state and local counter-UAS landscape. In December 2025, Congress enacted the SAFER SKIES Act as part of the FY2026 National Defense Authorization Act (Pub. L. 119-60, Division H, Title LXXXVI), amending 6 U.S.C. § 124n to extend counter-UAS detection and mitigation authority to state, local, tribal, and territorial law enforcement under a federal framework of authorized systems, training, and certification. FEMA now funds state, local, tribal, and territorial counter-UAS capability through the FY2027 Counter-UAS Grant Program, administered through each State Administrative Agency.
